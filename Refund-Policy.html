Return Policy

Easy returns and customer satisfaction

At Lebrostone, customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can request a return or replacement.

Return Eligibility

  • - Products must be returned within 7 days.
  • - Items must be unused and in original packaging.
  • - Damaged or incorrect products are eligible.

Non-Returnable Items

  • - Opened or used products.
  • - Damaged due to customer handling.
  • - Special sale items.

Return Process

Contact support with your order details to start a return request.

  • - Share Order ID and reason.
  • - Attach product images if needed.
  • - Review within 48 hours.

Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the product is damaged or incorrect.
