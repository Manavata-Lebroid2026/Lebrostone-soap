Shipping Policy

Fast and reliable delivery information

At Lebrostone, we ensure safe and timely delivery of your orders. This policy explains our shipping process and timelines.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after confirmation.

  • - Order Confirmation: You will receive confirmation after placing order.
  • - Dispatch Notification: Tracking details will be shared after shipping.

Delivery Time

  • - Metro Cities: 3–5 business days.
  • - Other Locations: 5–8 business days.
  • Delivery may delay due to weather or holidays.

Shipping Charges

Free shipping on selected orders. Charges (if any) shown at checkout.

Order Tracking

Tracking details will be sent via SMS or email after dispatch.

Failed Delivery

Orders may return if address is incorrect or delivery fails multiple times.

Damaged Packages

Do not accept damaged packages. Contact support immediately.
